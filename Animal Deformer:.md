Animal Deformer:

Generate a random full body photo-realistic image of a dog with no background by default

From a toolbar of 3 different brushes:

Grab brush: Grab a section of the image and push in the direction the cursor is dragged

Magnify brush: Distort the section of image within the brush radius by either zooming in or out 

Snake grab: like the grab brush but you can drag it further by cloning parts of in image

check box on the bottom to click on when you are done and the image becomes uneditable anymore.

It has one brush: the tip of a lit match stick

The main canvas is white
